## **Why I Love *Iron Man***
*Iron Man* is one of my all-time favorite movies because it blends **technology**, **heroism**, and **character growth** in a way that still feels fresh today.

---

## **Key Highlights**
1. **Robert Downey Jr.** delivers an iconic performance.
2. The movie kick-started the **Marvel Cinematic Universe**.
3. Incredible mix of **action**, **humor**, and **heart**.

## **What Makes It Special**
- A brilliant origin story  
- A flawed but lovable protagonist  
- Amazing suit-building sequences
